This is the recipe for making injera

Injera is a sourdough flatbread that is a staple in Ethiopian cuisine. It is made from teff flour and has a unique spongy texture.

Here is a simple recipe to make injera:

  1. Ingredients:
    • 2 cups teff flour
    • 3 cups water
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda (optional)
  2. Instructions:
    1. In a large bowl, mix the teff flour and water until smooth.
    2. Cover the bowl with a cloth and let it sit at room temperature for 2-3 days to ferment.
    3. Add salt and baking soda (if using) to the batter and mix well.
    4. Heat a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat.
    5. Pour a ladleful of batter onto the skillet and spread it into a circle.
    6. Cook for about 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface.
    7. Cover with a lid and cook for another minute.
    8. Remove from the skillet and let it cool on a plate.
    9. Repeat with the remaining batter.
