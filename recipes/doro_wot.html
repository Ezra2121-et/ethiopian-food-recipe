This is the recipe for making doro wot

Doro Wot is a spicy Ethiopian chicken stew that is traditionally served with injera. It is known for its rich flavor and vibrant color.

Here is a simple recipe to make doro wot:

  1. Ingredients:
    • 2 lbs chicken, cut into pieces
    • 2 large onions, finely chopped
    • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
    • 2 tablespoons berbere spice mix
    • 4 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 inch ginger, grated
    • Salt to taste
    • 1/4 cup water
  2. Instructions:
    1. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
    2. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
    3. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute.
    4. Add the berbere spice mix and cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
    5. Add the chicken pieces and stir to coat them with the spice mixture.
    6. Add salt and water, cover the pot, and simmer for 30-40 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
    7. Serve hot with injera.
