This is a recipe for making gomen

Gomen is a traditional Ethiopian dish made with collard greens. It is often served as a side dish with injera and other stews.

Here is a simple recipe to make gomen:

  1. Ingredients:
    • 1 lb collard greens, chopped
    • 1 large onion, finely chopped
    • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
    • 2 cloves garlic, minced
    • 1 inch ginger, grated
    • Salt to taste
    • 1/4 cup water
  2. Instructions:
    1. In a large pot, heat the vegetable oil over medium heat.
    2. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
    3. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute.
    4. Add the collard greens and stir to combine.
    5. Add salt and water, cover the pot, and simmer for 20-30 minutes until the greens are tender.
